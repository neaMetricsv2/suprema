# About Suprema South Africa

Suprema South Africa is an authorised partner of **Suprema Inc.** — a global leader in biometric security technology headquartered in South Korea and represented in over 140 countries. We bring Suprema's world-class product range to the South African market, backed by local implementation expertise, in-country technical support and an intimate understanding of the unique security and operational challenges faced by South African organisations.

## Who we are

We are a specialist biometric security company based in Johannesburg, serving enterprise, government, healthcare, retail and education customers across South Africa and into sub-Saharan Africa. Our team combines deep technical expertise in access control and workforce management with first-hand knowledge of the South African regulatory environment — including POPIA compliance and the unique demands of South Africa's diverse and challenging operating conditions.

## Why Suprema?

Suprema has been at the forefront of biometric technology since 2000, consistently ranking among the top biometric companies globally. Their products are trusted by Fortune 500 companies, national governments and world-class healthcare institutions because they deliver on three fundamental requirements:

- **Accuracy** — Suprema's fingerprint and face recognition algorithms consistently rank at the top of independent NIST evaluations
- **Reliability** — built to operate in extreme temperatures, humidity and harsh outdoor environments — well-suited to South African conditions
- **Scalability** — from a single door to a 1 000-door enterprise campus, the same platform manages it all

## Our local capability

We provide end-to-end project delivery — from site survey and system design through to installation, commissioning, training and ongoing maintenance. All support is provided from within South Africa, ensuring response times that meet the needs of mission-critical operations.

**Technical support:** Available Monday to Friday, 08:00–17:00 SAST, with emergency support arrangements available for critical infrastructure customers.
